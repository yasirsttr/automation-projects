**End-to-End Email Marketing & Lead Management Automation**

Project Overview: This project demonstrates a full-cycle, production-ready email marketing automation system from lead sourcing and enrichment to outreach execution, response handling, and CRM synchronization built to eliminate manual effort and ensure clean, scalable outbound operations.

**Problem:**
- The client faced multiple challenges in outbound email marketing: Lead sourcing, enrichment, outreach, and CRM updates were handled manually across tools
- No unified flow between lead data, email campaigns, and CRM
- Replies required manual review and classification
- This resulted in inefficiencies, delayed follow-ups, and inconsistent CRM data.

**Solution:** 
I designed and implemented an end-to-end automated outbound system that connects lead sourcing, email campaigns, reply handling, and CRM updates into a single streamlined workflow. The system automates the entire journey from raw lead data to qualified opportunities, while maintaining flexibility and data accuracy.

**Tools Used:**
- Clay – Lead sourcing, enrichment, and ICP filtering 
- Google Sheets – Lead staging and structured data handoff 
- n8n – Workflow orchestration, reply handling, and automation logic 
- SmartLead – Email outreach and campaign execution 
- Pipedrive – CRM synchronization and deal creation

**Key Features:**
- Automated Lead Sourcing & Enrichment
- ICP-based lead extraction and enrichment via Clay
- Clean, structured lead preparation before outreach
- Email Outreach Automation
- Automated lead push into SmartLead campaigns
- Reply Detection & Classification
- n8n workflows monitor inbox responses
- Automatic classification of replies (positive, neutral, negative)
- Qualified leads automatically pushed into Pipedrive
- Deal creation and pipeline updates without manual intervention

**Impact:**
- Significant reduction in manual lead handling and campaign setup
- Faster response handling and lead qualification
- Consistent CRM updates with zero manual syncing
- Improved visibility for sales teams into outreach performance
- Scalable outbound system ready for growth
